# Notice of Closure of the Moon/Nano Swap
Dear nano.trade users,

We regret to inform you that the Moon/Nano swap ([moon.nano.trade](https://moon.nano.trade "Go to moon.nano.trade")) has been discontinued and is now closed.

Reddit Moons launched on the Arbitrum Nova chain. Following the Arbitrum DAO's decision to minimize Nova, together with declining trading activity and shrinking support for Moons across major venues, we have decided to close this swap.

If you still hold Moons on Arbitrum Nova, they remain yours on-chain. Nova continues to operate, and you can still move or bridge your Moons to Arbitrum One or Ethereum through the [Arbitrum Portal](https://portal.arbitrum.io "Arbitrum Portal"), which stays accessible.

Please explore our other available swaps on [nano.trade](https://nano.trade "Go to nano.trade") and [banano.trade](https://banano.trade "Go to banano.trade").

Thank you for your continued support!
